Bookshelf+ Music

What's new in Music

Music in Bookshelf+ is now a place to discover, save, sing along with, and share favorite songs, from hymns to new releases. People who haven't added Music yet can listen to previews first.

What's new, screen by screen

Each screen, with the features that live on it.

Home

Playlists for You

Playlists hand-picked by Bookshelf+ appear right on Home, while Discover remains the place to explore by genre.

My Library

Personal and curated playlists

Playlists from Bookshelf+ sit alongside the ones people make themselves, with a label that tells them apart.

Favorites in one place

Everything favorited, from single songs to whole artists, is gathered together in My Library.

Artist page

Artist pages

Everything from an artist in one place: their songs, albums, and playlists, plus a little about who they are.

Favorite and share artists

Artists can be saved as favorites or shared with friends and family.

Album

Favorite individual songs

Any song can be saved as a favorite right from the album, without saving the whole album.

Share music

Songs, albums, artists, and playlists can all be shared with friends and family. For now, sharing covers playlists made by Bookshelf+.

Now Playing

Lyrics

Lyrics appear on screen while a song plays and follow along with the music, line by line.

Credits

Each song shows who sang, wrote, and arranged it. Especially meaningful for hymns, where the story behind the music matters.

Share a lyric passage

A meaningful line or two can be selected right in the lyrics and shared on its own.

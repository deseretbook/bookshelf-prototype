# Social marks: source and terms

`instagram.svg`, `facebook-01.svg` and `youtube.svg` are the single-color marks from Simple Icons (simple-icons 15.0.0), drawn from each owner's official brand resources:

- Instagram: https://about.meta.com/brand/resources/instagram
- Facebook: https://about.meta.com/brand/resources/facebook/logo
- YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/howyoutubeworks/resources/brand-resources/

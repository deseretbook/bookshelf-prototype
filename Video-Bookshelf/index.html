What's new in Video

Watch movies and short clips, faith and family content, inside Bookshelf. Full-length films for movie night, quick reels for any moment, and video classes to learn and grow, all in the same app as books, music, and podcasts.

What's new, screen by screen

Each screen, with the features that live on it. Screens are illustrative mockups; titles and artwork are placeholders.

Home

Movies

Full-length faith and family films, from comedies to true stories, with new titles right on Home.

Short Clips shelf

Bite-sized videos sit in their own shelf, ready for a quick watch.

Movie page

Extras

Trailers, behind-the-scenes looks, and other short videos live right on each movie's page.

Downloads

Movies can be downloaded ahead of time to watch without a connection.

Watching

Pick up where you left off

Movies already started show their progress and continue from the exact moment they were paused.

On the big screen

Videos can play on the TV with AirPlay, with captions available for every title.

Clips

Reels-style clips

Vertical clips play one after another. One swipe moves to the next, and any clip can lead to the full movie it came from.

Save and share clips

Favorite clips can be saved for later or sent to friends and family.

Class page

Video classes

Courses taught by trusted instructors, with concrete takeaways and spiritual insight for the real challenges of everyday life.

Lesson by lesson

Each class is broken into short lessons that can be watched in order. Progress is saved, so it's easy to continue with the next lesson.

Study guides

Classes can include a study guide to follow along, take notes, and go deeper after each lesson.

Download a whole class

An entire class can be downloaded at once to watch without a connection.

Music V1

Clickable iPhone prototype of the Bookshelf+ Music experience, built from the Figma “Music-V1” iOS 26 screens.

Try: My Library › Albums › This New Day › play a song. Open the mini player, use Up Next, add a song to a new playlist, or search “Gentri”.

Account › Offline Mode shows how songs that aren’t downloaded behave. Playback is simulated (no audio).
